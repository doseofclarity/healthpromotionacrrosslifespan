Family Health: The Core Unit

The Department of Health (DOH) uses a life span approach, viewing health care of individuals within the context of the family. The Family Health Office aims to improve survival, health and well-being across all stages: pre-pregnancy, prenatal, natal, postnatal, newborn, child, adolescent, adult and older person.

Key strategies: BEMOC (Basic Emergency Obstetric Care), IMCI, EPI, Healthy Lifestyle promotion.
Millennium Development Goals – MDG 4 (child mortality), MDG 5 (maternal health) remain priorities.

Maternal & Child Health

  • Maternal Health: At least 4 prenatal visits; TT immunization (Fully Immunized Mother); iron & folic acid supplementation; skilled birth attendance; BEMOC facilities.
  • Infant & Young Child Feeding: Exclusive breastfeeding for first 6 months; complementary feeding at 6 months while continuing breastfeeding up to 2 years.
  • Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI): Fully Immunized Child (BCG, DPT-HepB, OPV, Measles) before 1 year old. Hepatitis B at birth.
  • IMCI (Integrated Management of Childhood Illness): Assess, classify, treat for pneumonia, diarrhea, malnutrition, measles, etc.
  • Newborn Screening: R.A. 9288 – screening for metabolic disorders within 48 hours–2 weeks.

Adolescent & Youth Health (10–24 years)

Adolescents account for >30% of Philippine population. Leading causes of death: accidents & injuries (30.68/100,000). Key thrusts:

  • Adolescent-friendly health services & environments.
  • Fertility awareness, sexuality education, prevention of teenage pregnancy.
  • Substance abuse prevention: smoking prevalence 21%, alcohol 41%.
  • Reproductive health counseling, family planning for sexually active young people.
  • Nutrition, mental health, dental care package.
Essential package: management of illness, RTI/STI counseling, responsible sexual behavior.

Adult Health (25–59 years)

Leading causes of death: Cardiovascular diseases, cancers, COPD, diabetes – lifestyle-related. Major risk factors: smoking, physical inactivity, unhealthy diet, hypertension.

Women’s health: Breast cancer (#1), cervical cancer – promote BSE, Pap smear. High rates of goiter, thyroid disorders.
Men’s health: Higher mortality from accidents, chronic liver disease, TB. Prostate health: DRE, PSA.

Health promotion: Healthy lifestyle (non-smoking, balanced diet, physical activity), early screening for hypertension, diabetes, hypercholesterolemia.

Older Persons (60+ years)

6% of population (5 million). Double burden: degenerative + communicable diseases. Top causes: heart disease, vascular disease, cancer, pneumonia, COPD. Common geriatric problems: difficulty walking, chewing, visual/hearing impairment, osteoporosis, arthritis.

  • Essential health care package: management of chronic illness, nutrition counseling, dental care, screening for hypertension/diabetes.
  • R.A. 9257 (Expanded Senior Citizens Act): 20% discount, free medical and dental check-ups in government hospitals.
  • Community-based and institution-based models for elderly care.

FP, Nutrition & Oral Health

Family Planning: Contraceptive prevalence rate ~48.9% (2003). Methods: pills, injectables, IUD, condom, BTL, vasectomy, LAM, SDM, two-day method. Birth spacing 3-5 years reduces maternal & child mortality.

Nutrition Program: Micronutrient supplementation (Vitamin A for 6-71 months, iron for pregnant/lactating, iodine). Food Fortification Act (R.A. 8976). Promote Nutritional Guidelines for Filipinos.

Oral Health: Dental caries (92%) and periodontal disease (78%). Basic package: oral exam, prophylaxis, fluoride, ART, supervised toothbrushing.

PHN Role in Lifespan Health Promotion

  • Antenatal & postnatal: Provide prenatal counseling, TT immunization, micronutrients, breastfeeding support, postpartum visits (1st week and 6 weeks).
  • Child health: Supervise EPI, IMCI, growth monitoring, newborn screening, deworming, oral health education.
  • Adolescent: Organize youth-friendly health sessions, STI/HIV prevention, substance abuse education.
  • Adult: Screen for hypertension, diabetes, cancers (BSE/CBE, Pap smear), promote healthy lifestyle (smoking cessation, physical activity, diet).
  • Older persons: Facilitate access to senior citizen benefits, home visits, chronic disease management, fall prevention.
  • Use nursing process, home visit, community organizing, and referral system.
